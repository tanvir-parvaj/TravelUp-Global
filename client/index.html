📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Booking & Reservation Policy

All package and custom trip reservations made through TravelUp Global are subject to real-time confirmation. Rates include applicable taxes and designated included services.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Travelers may request cancellation through their Traveler Portal up to 7 days prior to departure for eligible refunds according to vendor partner terms.

3. Identity & Travel Documentation

Travelers are responsible for holding valid passports (minimum 6 months validity) and required visas for destination countries.

🛡️ Privacy & Data Security

1. Information Collection & Usage

TravelUp Global collects user information strictly for account authentication, itinerary generation, reservation processing, and personalized travel recommendations.

2. Encryption & Financial Security

All transactions processed via Stripe, PayPal, and SSLCommerz use bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store raw payment card credentials on our servers.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Personal data is strictly never sold or leased to third parties. Only essential reservation details are communicated to verified airlines and hotel partners.
